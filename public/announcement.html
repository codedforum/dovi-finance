How it works

  1. Type a handle or an emailAdd an amount. You never need their wallet address.
  2. They sign in onceA wallet is created for them and the dollars are theirs.
  3. Not claimed in time?It expires after 7 days. You can take it back in full, with no fee, and you can take it back earlier too.

What you can do today

  • Pay by handle or email. The money waits in an on-chain escrow until it is claimed.
  • Take it back. Before it is claimed, with no fee.
  • Pay from X. After you link X and turn it on, tweet @DoviFinance send 2 usdc to @name and DOVI sends it inside the limits you set. The reply has no link, and the recipient gets a direct message with the claim steps if their messages are open.
  • Pay many people at once. One list, one approval, with locked and scheduled payouts.
  • Ask in plain words. The money agent shows an exact preview and waits for your approval.
  • Let an AI agent pay for you. A send API with a hard dollar cap per payment and per day, and a key you can revoke.
  • Links, receipts and statements. Reusable payment links, shareable proof pages and monthly statements.
  • Withdraw. To your own Base wallet.

Not live yet, said plainly

  • Mainnet. DOVI runs on Base Sepolia with test USDC until it is proven. Nothing here moves real money.
  • Real naira. Paying naira for USDC and cashing out to a Nigerian bank both work on the test network today, with simulated naira. Real naira needs a licensed partner and mainnet.
  • Private send. Planned for the mainnet launch.
