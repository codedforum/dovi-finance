# DOVI Brand Pack

## Core idea

The DOVI mark combines a strong `D`, a portal, and two connected endpoints. It represents familiar login becoming a walletless dollar account, then value moving through controlled financial paths.

## Logo assets

- `dovi-mark.svg`: master square mark.
- `dovi-wordmark-dark.svg`: dark wordmark for light backgrounds.
- `dovi-wordmark-light.svg`: light wordmark for dark backgrounds.
- `dovi-app-icon-1024.png`: app-store master icon.
- `dovi-app-icon-512.png`: PWA large icon.
- `dovi-app-icon-192.png`: PWA small icon.
- `apple-touch-icon.png`: Apple touch icon.

Maintain clear space equal to one quarter of the mark width. Do not recolor individual logo elements, stretch, rotate, add shadows, or place the dark wordmark on a low-contrast background.

## Palette

- Evergreen: `#07130f`
- DOVI mint: `#24e47d`
- Warm paper: `#f3f1e9`
- Warm gold: `#ffe59b`
- Lilac: `#ded5ff`
- Pale blue: `#cde9ff`

Evergreen and warm paper are the structural colors. Mint signals movement, approval, and primary actions. Gold, lilac, and blue distinguish product/funding categories; they must not imply risk or regulatory status by color alone.

## Voice

- Clear before clever.
- Familiar money language, invisible blockchain mechanics.
- Never call stablecoins insured deposits.
- Never describe every transaction as free when FX or fiat-partner fees apply.
- Never imply that a risk card is investment advice or a safety guarantee.

## Primary lines

- Master: `Money that moves. Capital that builds.`
- Consumer: `A dollar account in your pocket.`
- Founder: `Raise in dollars. Receive capital as you deliver.`
- Backer: `Fund verified ideas with transparent terms and protected releases.`

## Image-generation provenance

The three raster masters were generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-08-28 using production prompts for: walletless global value movement, human/social-login onboarding, and protected multi-instrument global funding. Prompts prohibited text, third-party logos, flags, currency symbols, generic crypto coins, and watermarks.
