Help and safety

DOVI lets you send dollars to an X handle or an email on Base. The person claims the money by signing in. Here is how it works and how to stay safe.

How a payment works

  1. You type a handle or email and an amount, and review exactly what they receive and what you pay.
  2. You approve. The money is locked in a payment contract on Base, not held by DOVI.
  3. They open your link, sign in as that handle or email, and claim it.
  4. If they do not claim it in time, or you change your mind, you can take it back from Activity. You get everything back and no fee is charged.

Paying many people

Open Pay many people from Home. Paste one person per line, an X handle or an email and an amount, or set the same amount for everyone. DOVI checks every line, shows the total and the fee, and you approve once. Each person gets their own claim link, and you can copy the links, post them on X, or copy everything as a spreadsheet row list. Each payment can be taken back on its own until it is claimed. A batch holds up to 50 people.

Pay from X

Link your X account, then turn on Pay from X and set the most you allow per payment and per day. After that, tweeting @DoviFinance send 5 usdc to @name makes DOVI send it from your account and reply without a link. The recipient gets a direct message with the claim steps and link, and if their messages are closed they can still see the payment on Home after signing in with that X account. DOVI can only create payments you tweet, inside your limits, and it can never send money to itself. You can cancel any payment in Activity until it is claimed, and turn Pay from X off at any time, which also removes the allowance.

Withdrawing

Open Withdraw from Home, paste a Base wallet address you control, choose an amount, and approve. The money goes straight to that wallet and DOVI covers the network fee. A private route, which hides the link between your DOVI account and the wallet, opens at launch. It does not hide the amount or the timing, and DOVI can still see it. Cash out to naira works on the test network: choose Cash out to naira in Withdraw, enter your Nigerian bank details, and send test USDC. The naira payout is simulated, so no real money moves until a licensed partner and mainnet are in place.

What it costs

Sending to someone who already uses DOVI has no DOVI fee. Sending to someone new costs 1 percent with a minimum of $0.10, and the fee is only charged if they claim it. You always see the exact fee before you approve.

Beta limits

DOVI is in beta. Each payment, each day and the total amount held are capped to keep everyone safe. If you hit a limit, the app tells you which one.

Staying safe

  • DOVI will never ask for your password, a seed phrase or a private key. Never share them with anyone.
  • Check the handle before you approve. A first payment to a new handle shows a warning.
  • Claim links are meant for one person. Only share a link with the person you sent it to.
  • DOVI is not a bank. Balances are not insured deposits.

Something wrong?

Open DOVI, go to More, and choose Report a problem or a scam. Tell us what happened and we will look into it.
