What DOVI is

DOVI is a beta service that lets you hold USDC on Base in a wallet created for you, send it to an X handle or an email, and claim payments sent to you. DOVI does not hold your private keys. Payments to new recipients are held in a smart contract until they are claimed or taken back.

What DOVI is not

DOVI is not a bank and balances are not insured deposits. DOVI does not give investment, tax or legal advice.

Fees

A fee of 1 percent, with a minimum of $0.10, applies to a payment to a new recipient and is charged only when the payment is claimed. If a payment is taken back, no fee is charged. Sending to someone who already uses DOVI has no DOVI fee. Network costs may apply and are shown when they do.

Limits and expiry

Beta limits apply per payment, per day and in total. A payment that is not claimed expires. After it expires, or at any time before it is claimed, the sender can take it back.

Your responsibilities

  • Check the recipient before you approve. Payments on a blockchain cannot be reversed once claimed.
  • Keep your sign in methods safe.
  • Use DOVI lawfully. Do not use it for fraud, harassment or to avoid sanctions.

Risks

This is new software. Bugs, network problems and stablecoin issues can cause delays or loss. Use only amounts you can afford to lose while DOVI is in beta.

Changes and contact

We may change these terms and will post changes here. To reach us, use the report option inside the app.
