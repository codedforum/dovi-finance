Privacy

Beta version, last updated 18 October 2026. A reviewed version will replace it before DOVI opens more widely.

What we collect

  • Your sign in identity: your email, your Google account id, or your X account id and handle.
  • Your wallet address on Base.
  • Payment records: amounts, recipients you typed, status, and notes you add.
  • Basic technical data such as your IP address for security and rate limits.

What we do not collect

We never see or store your private keys, your password or a seed phrase. Your wallet is created and protected by Coinbase through its embedded wallet service.

How we use it

To run your account, show your activity, deliver notifications and emails about your payments, prevent abuse, and meet legal duties. We do not sell your data.

What is public

Payments on Base are public on the blockchain. A claim link page shows only the amount, the sender display name, the recipient label and the status.

Who we share with

Service providers that run DOVI, such as Coinbase for wallets and card purchases, our email provider, and infrastructure hosts. They only get what they need to do their job.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. To ask about your data, use the report option inside the app.
