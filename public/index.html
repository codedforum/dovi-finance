How it works

From a handle to dollars in hand.

DOVI turns a login you already have into a self custodial dollar account. The blockchain work stays invisible and you stay in control.

1

Sign in, get a wallet

Continue with a login you already have. DOVI creates a walletless account behind it. There is no seed phrase to write down and nothing to install.

2

Send to a handle

Type an X handle or an email, review the amount and the fee, and approve. The money waits safely until they claim it, and you can take it back.

3

They claim with one login

They open your link, sign in with X or email, and the dollars land in their new DOVI account. They never need a wallet first.

Made for people, not wallets

Money that feels like sending a message.

No extension and no seed phrase. You sign in the way you already do and DOVI handles the chain work out of sight.

Security

Custody you can feel, control you can see.

Walletless by design

Your account is created from a familiar login. There is no seed phrase to lose and no extension to install.

You stay in control

You review and approve every send. If you turn on Pay from X or make an agent key, DOVI sends only inside the limits you set, and you can switch it off at any time.

Keys stay with Coinbase

Your wallet is created and protected by Coinbase's embedded wallet service. DOVI never sees or stores a private key.

Questions

What people ask first.

What if they never claim it?

The claim expires after 7 days and the money returns to you in full, with no fee. You can also reclaim it yourself at any time before it is claimed.

What does it cost?

Sending to someone already on DOVI has no DOVI fee. Sending to a new handle or email costs 1 percent, minimum $0.10, paid by you on top, and only if they claim. The review screen shows the exact total before you approve.

Do I need a crypto wallet?

No. You sign in with email, Google or X and DOVI creates the account. There is no seed phrase and nothing to install. The person you pay does not need one either.

Can DOVI move my money without me?

Only inside limits you set yourself. A normal send needs your approval. If you turn on Pay from X or create an agent key, DOVI sends within the per payment and daily limits you chose, and you can switch them off or revoke a key at any time.

Is my balance insured?

No. Balances are dollar stablecoins on Base, not bank deposits, and they are not insured. The beta has low limits.

Which chains and currencies?

During the beta, test USDC on the Base Sepolia test network. Other chains and local currencies appear only after a real small send is proven.
